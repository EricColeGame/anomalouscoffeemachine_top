## Why an Unnamed Character Can Work Better

From a writing standpoint, an unnamed character is a deliberate tool, not an oversight. It creates three effects at once.

First, it preserves tension. Every conversation becomes a slow reveal, and the player keeps playing to learn more. Second, it makes the character a mirror — players project their own read onto her, which is why community interpretations vary so much. Third, it keeps the focus on the interaction rather than the label. The game is about what you do at the machine, not about a nameplate.

That design choice explains why the Anomalous Coffee Machine character name remains an open question years after release. It is supposed to be.
