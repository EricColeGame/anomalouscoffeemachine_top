## Progression Fundamentals: How the Game Gates You

Anomalous Coffee Machine does not hand you a quest log. Progression is conversational. You advance by paying attention, varying your inputs, and letting scenes finish. That structure is deliberate, but it also means a missed line can look like a hard wall.

Based on player experience shared across community threads, progression gates tend to fall into a few recognizable categories:

| Gate type | Typical trigger | Player-reported approach |
|---|---|---|
| Combination gate | A specific drink order is required | Change one variable at a time, not three |
| Conversation gate | A topic needs revisiting later | Re-engage after new dialogue appears |
| Patience gate | A scene needs to play out fully | Let text finish; do not click through |
| Version gate | Content only exists in a newer build | Update, then replay the triggering scene |

A few habits that consistently help:

- **Change one thing per attempt.** Swapping multiple ingredients at once makes it impossible to know which change mattered.
- **Re-read before you re-order.** The girl's wording often shifts subtly when you are close to a trigger.
- **Keep a written log.** A simple list of orders and outcomes beats memory once you pass a dozen attempts.
- **Finish scenes completely.** Skipping text is the most commonly reported cause of a "broken" progression flag.

None of this is glamorous, but it converts guesswork into a process — and a process is what gets you unstuck.

## Tracking Updates Without Spoiling the Story

The tension with update tracking is obvious: patch notes tell you what changed, but they also tell you what exists. If you want to preserve discovery, track updates for *stability* rather than content.

| Source | Reliability | Best used for |
|---|---|---|
| Official itch.io project page | High | Confirming the current build and downloads |
| Developer devlogs and posts | High | Understanding what changed and why |
| Community forums and Discord servers | Medium | Troubleshooting, shared discoveries |
| Video walkthroughs | Medium | Seeing a progression path in motion |

Community reports are genuinely useful, but treat them as leads, not facts. A tip written for an older build can send you chasing a trigger that no longer exists — or worse, one that was never real. When a community tip and the official page disagree, trust the official page.

## Common Progression Blockers and How to Fix Them

Most stalls are not bugs. They are mismatches between what the game expects and what you are doing. Here is a troubleshooting table built from the most frequently reported symptoms:

| Symptom | Likely cause | Suggested fix |
|---|---|---|
| Story stops advancing entirely | Outdated build or missed trigger | Update first, then replay the last scene |
| Save fails to load | Version mismatch between save and build | Restore backup, update, retest |
| Dialogue keeps repeating | Trigger already fired | Try a different order or approach |
| Text advances too fast to read | Text speed setting | Slow it down in options |
| Scene freezes mid-conversation | Known bug in older build | Check for a newer upload |

Work through that table in order. Updating is almost always the first move, because it eliminates the largest variable and costs you nothing but a download.

## FAQ

**Is the Anomalous Coffee Machine latest version different from the sequel?**
Yes. The Anomalous Coffee Machine latest version refers to the most recent build of the original game, while Anomalous Coffee Machine 2 is a separate follow-up project. Updating the original will not give you sequel content, and the two are downloaded independently.

**How do I know if an update broke my save file?**
The most reliable test is to back up your save, update the game, and load your most recent file. If it loads but a scene misbehaves, that is usually a trigger issue rather than corruption. If it fails to load at all, restore your backup and report the problem to the developer.

**Do I need to restart my playthrough after updating?**
Usually not. Most players report that existing saves continue to work after an update. A restart is only worth considering if you were stuck on a scene that the new build specifically addressed — replaying that scene from an earlier save is often enough.

**Where should I check for the newest build?**
The official itch.io project page maintained by HoruBrain is the authoritative source. Following the project there gives you the best chance of catching the Anomalous Coffee Machine latest version the moment it goes live.
