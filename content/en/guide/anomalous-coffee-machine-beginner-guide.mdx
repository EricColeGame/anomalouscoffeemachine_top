## What Is Anomalous Coffee Machine?

You are standing behind a counter. A girl you have never met sits across from you. Between the two of you sits a coffee machine that refuses to behave the way a coffee machine should.

That is the setup for **Anomalous Coffee Machine**, a short interactive visual novel and simulation hybrid from developer HoruBrain, playable on the [official Anomalous Coffee Machine page on itch.io](https://horubrain.itch.io/anomalous-coffee-machine). If you are about to start your first run, this Anomalous Coffee Machine beginner guide will spare you a few wasted attempts.

The game hands you almost no instructions. There is no tutorial pop-up, no objective marker, and no obviously "correct" answer. What it gives you instead is a conversation, a machine, and a customer whose reactions are the only feedback you get. That is also why this Anomalous Coffee Machine beginner guide focuses on habits rather than secret codes — the drinks, dialogue branches, and endings are meant to be discovered. Everything below is about approaching the game so that discovery stays fun instead of frustrating.

## How the Core Loop Works

Every run follows the same rhythm: read, respond, brew, watch, repeat. Understanding that rhythm is the single biggest advantage a beginner can have, because it turns a confusing first playthrough into a series of small, deliberate experiments.

| Phase | What You Do | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Read the scene | Follow the dialogue and the mood of the conversation | Hints about what she expects are buried in the wording |
| 2. Choose a response | Pick one of the dialogue options on screen | Your answer nudges the conversation and, eventually, the ending |
| 3. Brew the drink | Interact with the machine and its ingredients | Brewing is the game's main feedback mechanic |
| 4. Watch the reaction | Study how she responds to the cup | Her reaction is your only reliable scoreboard |
| 5. Repeat | Loop back into the conversation | Each cycle pushes the scene toward a different outcome |

The loop is deliberately short, and that is good news for new players. Short cycles mean faster feedback, and faster feedback means you learn the game's rules by testing them rather than by reading about them.

If you follow only one piece of advice from this Anomalous Coffee Machine beginner guide, make it step six. Treating the first run as a scouting trip removes most of the pressure that makes new players freeze up at the machine.

## Anomalous Coffee Machine Beginner Guide: Core Tips for New Players

Here are the habits that separate a smooth first hour from a frustrating one:

- **Slow down on dialogue.** The conversation is the puzzle, not a cutscene between brewing attempts.
- **Change one variable at a time.** If you alter the drink and your response at once, you will never know which one caused the reaction.
- **Track reactions, not outcomes.** A cold response is information, not a failure state.
- **Replay on purpose.** Instead of restarting randomly, pick one thing you want to test.
- **Stop chasing a "perfect" run.** In most short visual novels, the interesting content lives in the imperfect branches.
- **Avoid spoiler-heavy walkthroughs early.** Community reports often describe the endings as the whole point of the experience.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Pay attention to her tone and word choice | Skip dialogue to reach the machine faster |
| Change one thing per attempt | Randomly mash every available option |
| Replay after finishing a run | Assume a bad reaction means the run is ruined |
| Check content notes before playing | Assume online hints match your version of the game |
| Keep notes on what worked | Expect the game to explain itself |

Written notes sound excessive for a short game, but they are genuinely useful here. A quick list of "what I brewed" and "how she reacted" turns guesswork into a pattern you can actually read.

## Dialogue Choices, Endings, and Replay Value

Dialogue is where most beginners lose their bearings. The options usually look small — a tone shift, a question, a pause — but they compound over a run. The table below breaks down the kinds of choices you will meet and how to handle them.

| Choice Type | What It Usually Affects | How to Approach It |
|---|---|---|
| Tone of reply | How open she is on the next line | Match the mood of the scene rather than your own curiosity |
| Curiosity vs. caution | How much you learn about her | Ask questions early, while the conversation is still loose |
| Brewing decisions | Her reaction to the cup | Adjust one element per attempt |
| Silence or hesitation | The pacing of the conversation | Use sparingly — it can read as indifference |

Community reports suggest the game includes more than one outcome and that some endings are easier to stumble into than others. Because the exact conditions are part of the experience, most players deliberately avoid publishing full solutions. That is worth respecting on your first few runs.

### Common Beginner Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | Better Approach |
|---|---|---|
| Rushing the dialogue | You miss the cues that drive the scene | Read each line once for meaning, once for tone |
| Brewing randomly | You learn nothing from the result | Change one ingredient or step at a time |
| Quitting after a bad reaction | You lose the context for what went wrong | Finish the run, then adjust |
| Assuming one "true" ending | You ignore most of the content | Explore branches on purpose |
| Reading full spoilers immediately | It removes the discovery loop | Save walkthroughs for a stuck moment |

None of these mistakes are fatal. The game is short enough that a "wasted" run still teaches you something about how the machine and the conversation interact.

## FAQ

**Do I need to play anything before starting Anomalous Coffee Machine?**
No. The game stands on its own, and the opening scene gives you everything you need to begin. Just go in expecting to learn by experimenting rather than by following instructions.

**How long does a first run take?**
Community reports describe individual runs as short and easy to replay, which is exactly why a beginner guide recommends finishing one run before judging the game. The value comes from the second and third attempts, not the first.

**Is there a correct way to brew the coffee?**
There is no universal answer, and that is intentional. Change one variable at a time, watch how she reacts, and treat every reaction as usable information rather than a pass-or-fail grade.

**Where should I go for updates or the sequel?**
The developer's official itch.io pages are the most reliable source for both the original game and Anomalous Coffee Machine 2, including any content notes or version changes.

The best way to use an Anomalous Coffee Machine beginner guide is to read it once, then put it aside. The game is short, strange, and built around paying attention to another person — and that is something no walkthrough can do for you.
