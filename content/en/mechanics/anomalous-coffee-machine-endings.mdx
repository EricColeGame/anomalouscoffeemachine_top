Few indie visual novels hide their true nature as well as Anomalous Coffee Machine, the short interactive simulation from developer HoruBrain in which a mysterious girl sits across from you and asks for a cup of coffee. What looks like a cozy barista sim quickly becomes a puzzle about cause and effect: every ingredient you drop into the machine changes the conversation, the mood, and the outcome you walk away with. That is why Anomalous Coffee Machine endings matter so much — they are the scoreboard for a game that never explains its own rules. If you have finished one route and wondered how many Anomalous Coffee Machine endings you are still missing, this guide breaks down how the system works and how to hunt down the rest.

## How Anomalous Coffee Machine Endings Are Decided

The game hands you three things: a machine, a set of ingredients, and a guest. There is no dialogue wheel, no morality meter on screen, and no obvious "correct" answer. Instead, the outcome is calculated from what you serve and how you behave while serving it.

Player experience consistently describes the same core loop. Each ingredient you add nudges hidden values — call them anomaly, trust, and patience — and the ending is chosen when the cup is finished. Because those values are invisible, the only reliable way to learn the system is to change one variable at a time and watch what happens.

### The Ingredient-to-Outcome Loop

Think of each serving as a small experiment:

- **Baseline ingredients** keep the scene stable and tend to produce grounded, conversational endings.
- **Unusual ingredients** push the anomaly value upward, which unlocks stranger scenes and more extreme conclusions.
- **Sequencing matters.** Adding the same items in a different order can produce a different result, which is why two players can swear they served the "same" coffee and still got different endings.

### Does the Game Track Your Endings?

Yes — the first game and its sequel both keep an internal record of what you have seen, usually surfaced through a gallery or ending list on the title screen. That list is your real progress bar. If a slot is still blank, the game is telling you a branch exists that you have not triggered yet.

| Factor | What it influences | Player-facing signal |
|---|---|---|
| Ingredient choice | The main anomaly value and scene branch | The girl's reaction and dialogue tone |
| Ingredient order | Which branch fires when values tie | Sudden shifts mid-conversation |
| Conversation responses | Trust and patience values | How open or guarded she becomes |
| Serving speed / pacing | Whether a scene escalates or stalls | The machine's behavior and sound cues |
| Repeat visits | Whether a hidden branch becomes available | New options or prompts on later runs |

### Why the Order You Play In Matters

Players who chase the strangest endings first often report that the calmer routes feel different afterward — the girl's dialogue carries new weight once you know what the machine can do. If you care about the story landing properly, work from grounded to extreme rather than the reverse.

## A Repeatable Workflow for Hunting Endings

Random guessing burns hours. A structured approach turns ending hunting into a checklist exercise.

| Step | Action | Why it helps |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Check the gallery first | Shows exactly how many endings remain |
| 2 | Pick one unclaimed slot | Keeps your test focused |
| 3 | Change only one ingredient | Isolates the variable that matters |
| 4 | Note the reaction mid-scene | Early cues tell you if you are on track |
| 5 | Save before serving | Lets you retry without replaying |
| 6 | Log the result | Builds a personal combination chart |
| 7 | Repeat with the next slot | Systematic coverage beats luck |

### Keep a Simple Ending Log

A plain text file or spreadsheet with three columns — ingredients, order, result — will do more for your completion rate than any guide. After a dozen runs you will start seeing patterns, such as which ingredients reliably push a scene toward escalation and which ones reset it.

### Use Save Points Aggressively

Most players underestimate how much time reloading saves. If the game supports saving mid-scene, save immediately before the final serve. That single habit can cut the time needed to see the remaining Anomalous Coffee Machine endings roughly in half, according to community reports.

## Common Mistakes That Cost You Endings

| Mistake | What happens | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Repeating the same "safe" cup | You loop into the same grounded ending | Deliberately push one value to its extreme |
| Changing several ingredients at once | You cannot tell which one mattered | Isolate one variable per run |

| Quitting after a "bad" ending | You miss story context | Treat every ending as data |

### Don't Ignore the Quiet Scenes

The most easily missed Anomalous Coffee Machine endings are often the least dramatic ones. When the girl goes silent or the machine hums without prompting, that is usually the game signaling that a branch is about to open. Responding calmly in those moments unlocks routes that aggressive ingredient stacking never reaches.

## Tips for Seeing Every Ending Without Burning Out

- **Rotate goals.** Alternate between a targeted hunt and a relaxed run so the game stays fun.
- **Set a session limit.** Three or four attempts per sitting is plenty; fatigue leads to sloppy notes.
- **Compare with other players.** Community threads often confirm whether a combination is real or a fluke.
- **Revisit old saves.** Some endings only trigger after a specific earlier scene has been seen.
- **Accept ambiguity.** Part of the appeal is that the machine never fully explains itself.

The reason Anomalous Coffee Machine endings stay interesting is that the game treats them as a conversation rather than a checklist. You are meant to experiment, get strange results, and then try to explain them to yourself.

## FAQ

**How many Anomalous Coffee Machine endings are there?**
The exact count varies by how you define a distinct ending, and community reports differ on the total. Rather than chasing a number, use the in-game gallery as your reference — whatever it lists is the real target.

**Can you unlock every ending in a single playthrough?**
No. The endings branch from incompatible states, so multiple runs are required. Saving before the final serve is the closest thing to a shortcut.

**Do the first game and the sequel share endings?**
They are separate games with separate galleries, though the sequel references outcomes from the original. Playing the first game first makes the sequel's Anomalous Coffee Machine endings easier to interpret.

**What is the fastest way to find a missing ending?**
Identify the one value you have never pushed to an extreme — usually trust or anomaly — and build a single run around it. Change nothing else, and log the result.

Whether you are chasing a single elusive route or methodically clearing the gallery, the endings in Anomalous Coffee Machine reward patience more than cleverness. Serve deliberately, watch closely, and let the machine surprise you.
