If you are staring at the machine for the first time and wondering whether your choices actually matter, they do — and this **Anomalous Coffee Machine tutorial** will walk you through the setup, the serving loop, the dialogue decisions that shape the story, and the habits that separate a smooth playthrough from a frustrating one.

There is no combat, no timer pressure, and no inventory management to master — the challenge is entirely about paying attention.

## The Core Loop: Reading the Customer

The gameplay loop is deceptively simple. You are presented with a request, you choose how to respond, and the customer reacts. That reaction is the real feedback system, and it is where most players stop paying attention.

Watch for three things: changes in sentence length, changes in how directly she addresses you, and changes in what she asks for next. A customer who starts speaking in short, clipped lines is usually signaling that your last choice landed badly. A customer who volunteers extra detail is usually inviting you to push further.

| Cue | Likely Meaning | Suggested Response |
|---|---|---|
| She repeats a word from your last line | The topic interests her | Stay on that thread one more beat |
| Answers get shorter and flatter | You pushed too hard or too fast | Back off and offer a neutral option |
| She asks a question back | She is testing your intent | Answer honestly rather than cleverly |
| The drink request changes mid-scene | A branch has opened | Serve the new request before continuing dialogue |
| She goes quiet for a beat | A pause, not a failure | Wait and let the next prompt appear |

This is the single most useful habit in the entire game: respond to her, not to the menu. Players who chase the "correct" option tend to miss the emotional logic that drives the story forward.

## A Step-by-Step Anomalous Coffee Machine Tutorial Walkthrough Approach

You do not need a rigid walkthrough to enjoy the game, but you do need a method. This five-phase approach works whether you are on your first run or your fifth.

**Phase 1 — Explore everything.** On your first playthrough, deliberately choose the options you find least obvious. Curiosity maps the branches faster than caution does.

**Phase 2 — Finish the run.** Even a "bad" ending tells you something. Do not reload mid-scene unless you are testing a single variable; finishing gives you the full shape of the branch.

**Phase 3 — Log your choices.** Write down what you served and what you said at each major prompt. Two or three lines per scene is plenty.

**Phase 4 — Replay with one change.** Change a single decision per run. Changing five things at once makes it impossible to tell which choice caused the different outcome.

**Phase 5 — Hunt the remaining outcomes.** Once you know the branch points, you can target specific scenes instead of replaying from the start.

| Phase | Goal | Time Investment |
|---|---|---|
| Explore | See what the game is willing to show you | First full run |
| Finish | Understand the shape of one branch | Same run, no reloads |
| Log | Build a personal map of decision points | 5–10 minutes of notes |
| Replay | Isolate one variable per run | Short repeat sessions |
| Complete | Reach the endings you have not seen | Final cleanup runs |

Community reports suggest that most of the game's variation happens in a handful of key conversations rather than being spread evenly, which is exactly why single-variable replays are so efficient.

## Advanced Tips: Common Mistakes and Better Habits

Experienced players tend to make the same handful of errors when they first pick up a story-driven game like this one. Here is how to sidestep them.

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | Better Approach |
|---|---|---|
| Clicking through dialogue quickly | You miss the cue that unlocks the next branch | Read every line at least once |
| Treating the drink menu as the only choice | Dialogue choices often carry more weight | Weight words and drinks equally |
| Reloading after every "wrong" answer | You never learn how branches resolve | Let the scene play out |
| Assuming there is one canon ending | The game is built for multiple outcomes | Treat each ending as valid |
| Ignoring the sequel's differences | Muscle memory from the first game can mislead you | Re-learn the pacing in the sequel |

One more advanced trick: when you find a scene that feels unusually slow, do not skip it. Slow scenes are usually where the game is doing its heaviest narrative lifting — and where a single line can reframe everything you thought you understood about the customer.

## Anomalous Coffee Machine 2: What Changes

The sequel keeps the same counter, the same visual-novel structure, and the same mysterious-girl premise, but it leans harder into the strange, fictional-simulation side of the formula. If you are following this Anomalous Coffee Machine tutorial before jumping into the second game, the transferable skills are the same: read carefully, log your choices, and change one variable at a time.

What does not transfer is your specific branch knowledge. Do not assume that a decision that worked in the first game will produce the same result in the sequel. Treat it as a fresh map with familiar terrain.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Anomalous Coffee Machine hard to finish without a guide?**
No. It is a short, dialogue-driven experience with no reflex-based challenges. A tutorial helps mainly with efficiency — knowing how to track branches and avoid wasted replays — rather than with difficulty.

**How many endings does the game have?**
The game is designed around multiple outcomes, and community reports vary on the exact count as the developer continues to update it. The practical answer is that you will see meaningful variation by replaying with different dialogue and drink choices, so plan for at least a few runs.

**Do my drink choices actually matter, or is it all dialogue?**
Both matter. Drink selection often sets the tone of a scene, while dialogue choices determine how the customer responds to it. Ignoring either half of the loop will cost you branches.

**Should I play the sequel first?**
Play the original first if you can. The sequel builds on the same premise and pacing, and understanding the first game's rhythm makes the second one far easier to read. This Anomalous Coffee Machine tutorial covers everything you need for the original, and the same habits carry over directly.

## The Bottom Line

The best way to approach this game is with curiosity instead of a checklist. Serve something strange, say the honest thing, let the scene finish, and write down what happened. Do that a few times and you will have seen more of the story than most players ever do — and you will have earned it yourself rather than copied it from a walkthrough.
